## Current Program Logger: Siglent SPD1305X

- Add SPD1305X VISA mode with identity checking, exclusive VISA lock, refusal to take over an active output/timer, verified configuration before output enable, local 2-wire sense, and separate current/voltage measurements.
- Quantize current down to 1 mA steps without exceeding the requested setpoint. Commands below 1 mA become zero; explicit sub-1 mA nonzero recipe targets/initial settings are rejected on new-run start. Suppress unchanged quantized commands. Show a persistent warning about low-current accuracy and non-guaranteed rates.
- Shutdown attempts output off, timer off, current zero, and voltage zero independently, then verifies output/timer off and zero setpoints. Communication failure can prevent shutdown; this is not a hardware watchdog.
- Time-based steps, readout and plots are supported. Resistance-terminated steps are blocked for this supply because their existing <=5 ms confirmation gap is incompatible with the ~10 ms query-pair time observed in a limited no-load test. XRD short/open-circuit interlocks are not implemented by this adapter.
- Authorized no-load test on 2026-09-14: 1 V ceiling, 1–2 mA setpoints, 0 mA / 0.996–0.999 V readback, no instrument errors, final zero setpoints and output off verified. No wire/XRD sample was energized; current accuracy and loaded transients remain unvalidated.
