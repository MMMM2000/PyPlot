Current Program Logger finite Keithley trials now use a 190 ohm heating-to-cooling target with a separate 195 ohm output-off ceiling active only at heating currents. The batch requests 1 kHz CSV logging during early heating and the first two seconds of cooling, 100 Hz for prolonged heating, and 20 Hz for later cooling. Trial metadata records the requested logging schedule and actual current-command times; measurement timestamps now refer to completed reads.
