### Added

- TMA review and Analysis exports show signed heating strain, cooling strain and
  residual strain at the recorded cooling endpoint, with the common reference
  and cooling endpoint information. Missing or aborted cooling remains unknown.
- Repeated sweeps and first overheating retain separate derived values and
  export exclusions; zero-current endpoints and high-current holds are included.

### Fixed

- Failed CA/TMA review saves keep the editor choices and queue position and show
  an error instead of advancing or incrementing the saved revision.
